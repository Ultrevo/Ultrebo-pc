# Contributing

Ultrebo is designed and maintained by Ultrevo.

- **Bug reports and ideas are welcome.** Open an [issue](../../issues) or ask in the
  [Discord server](https://discord.gg/mAKGfaAWWW). Please include your operating system and version, and what you did.
- **Code contributions (pull requests) are not being accepted for now.** This keeps the project's licensing
  and direction simple. You are free to fork it under the terms of the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE).
